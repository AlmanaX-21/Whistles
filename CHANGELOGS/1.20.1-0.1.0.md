# Train Whistles 1.20.1-0.1.0

## Added

- Pull-chain controls for playing train whistles and horns.
- Configurable pitch range, master volume, hearing range, lever response, and release timing.
- Per-post whistle and horn sound settings.
- Custom whistle playback for automatic station arrivals.
- Deterministic playback from up to three sound sources on each train.
- Resource-pack discovery for custom train sounds.
- Compatibility with Tramways whistle signs.
